About Lia

Lia doesn’t start with a set of exercises. She starts with you — how you stand, breathe, turn and carry yourself.

Years of working with bodies of every age and history have taught her that the same exercise can help one person and do nothing for the next. So every session brings together Pilates, mobility and neuromovement in the proportions you need, with the attention to detail that only one-to-one work allows.

The aim is simple: to help you feel stronger, move more freely and trust your body in everyday life.

How we work

Each discipline has its own purpose. Your sessions combine them in whatever balance your body needs right now.

  • Breath and alignment woven through every exercise

Mobility

Freedom you can use.

Improve the range, control and confidence of everyday movement.

Explore Mobility
  • Hips, spine, shoulders and ankles — the joints you use all day
  • Controlled rotations and 90/90 work, never forced stretching
  • Range you can actually control, not range for show
  • Simple drills you can carry into your week

Neuromovement

Reconnect. Re-educate. Rebalance.

Use intentional movement to challenge coordination, awareness and the brain–body connection.

Explore Neuromovement
  • Balance and single-leg control
  • Cross-body and contralateral coordination patterns
  • Awareness of where your body is in space
  • Small, focused tasks that make big movements feel easier

The LIA Method

Not a class.Your session.

Every session is designed around one person: you. What we do on a given day depends on how you’re moving, how you feel and where you want to go.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Understand how you move.

  2. 02

    Understand

    Identify where movement feels restricted, weak or disconnected.

  3. 03

    Move

    Combine Pilates, mobility and neuromovement appropriately.

  4. 04

    Progress

    Adapt the work as your body changes.

This is not a rigid programme.

It is an evolving movement practice.
