# Image direction

## Mobility — critical correction
Do NOT reproduce the extreme backbend from the concept board. Show Lia coaching a normal adult through controlled 90/90 hip rotation, ankle dorsiflexion, thoracic rotation, shoulder CARs, or another realistic joint-control drill. No splits, contortion, gymnastics, impossible anatomy, or yoga spectacle.

## Neuromovement
Show one-to-one coordination, balance, proprioception or cross-body movement. It should look intelligent and movement-led, not medical or futuristic.

## Pilates
Use anatomically correct reformer or mat work: footwork, bridge, controlled leg extension, side-lying work, or core control. Equipment mechanics must make sense.

## Overall
Warm natural daylight, residential Jumeirah Park studio, editorial but believable, varied adult clients, no commercial-gym aesthetic. Lia should often be visibly coaching rather than merely posing.
