# Claude build brief — LIA

Build a premium responsive website for **LIA — Pilates • Mobility • Neuromovement**, a private home movement studio in Jumeirah Park, Dubai. The experience should feel private, sophisticated, warm, highly experienced, calm and deeply personalised — not like a gym, generic Pilates franchise, physiotherapy clinic or influencer site.

Hero: **MOVE BETTER / LIVE FULLER**. Copy: “A deeply personalised approach to movement, designed around your body, your goals and the way you live.” CTAs: **BOOK A SESSION** and **DISCOVER THE METHOD**.

Core disciplines: Pilates = strength/control/precision; Mobility = usable range and controlled joint movement; Neuromovement = coordination/awareness/brain-body connection. Keep the imagery visibly distinct. Mobility MUST show realistic controlled mobility such as 90/90 hip work, not extreme flexibility.

Include: philosophy strip; editorial movement statement; three discipline cards; “NOT A CLASS. YOUR SESSION.” method section (Observe → Understand → Move → Progress); understated experience section; private studio section; inclusive “who it’s for”; testimonials; final booking CTA.

Use the supplied SVG branding/icons and palette. Headline style: high-contrast editorial serif; body: clean modern sans. Lots of off-white space, charcoal typography, restrained olive accents. Responsive layouts must be intentionally redesigned for iPhone portrait/landscape, iPad, laptop and desktop — no horizontal overflow.

Read IMAGE-DIRECTION.md before selecting or generating photography. The reference board is visual direction only; its Mobility image is explicitly NOT approved.
